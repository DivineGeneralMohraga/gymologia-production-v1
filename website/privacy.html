3.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device tokens: Push notification tokens (Firebase FCM) to deliver gym and platform notifications to your device.
  • Location: Approximate location is collected only if you grant permission, used solely for gym search and ranking by proximity. We do not track your location continuously.
  • Attendance records: Marked by gym staff, stored against your membership.
  • Chat messages: Messages are encrypted at rest. We do not read your personal chat conversations.
  • Security logs: IP addresses, request timestamps, and security event markers for fraud prevention and platform security.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity via OTP.
  • To provide the CRM, attendance, and membership management services to gym owners and staff.
  • To display your fitness progress and exercise history within the app.
  • To process gym subscription payments through Cashfree Payments.
  • To send you notifications from your gym or from Gymologia (you can manage notification preferences in the app).
  • To verify gym owner KYC documents and activate gym profiles.
  • To process referral rewards and payouts via UPI.
  • To improve the Platform, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard and beyond-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS / TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your device and our servers.
  • AES-256-GCM encryption at rest for all sensitive documents including Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, and UPI IDs. Only masked representations (last 4 digits) are accessible to staff.
  • OTP codes are hashed using a one-way algorithm and never stored in plaintext. Codes expire quickly and cannot be reused.
  • Session tokens are stored as hashes. Sessions can be remotely invalidated from your account settings.
  • IP-based attack detection and automatic blocking for repeated unauthorized access attempts, OTP abuse, and SQL injection probes.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures staff can only access data their role permits.

While we take every reasonable precaution, no system is completely invulnerable. Please report any security concerns to support@gymologia.com.

7. Data Retention

  • Account and profile data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • After an account deletion request, your data is anonymized within 30 days of the deletion being processed.
  • Transaction records and financial data may be retained for a longer period as required by Indian tax laws (GST compliance, income tax records).
  • Security event logs are retained for a limited period (typically 90 days) for fraud investigation purposes.
  • KYC documents submitted by gym owners are retained as required by applicable laws and for compliance purposes.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Correct inaccurate or outdated information via your profile settings in the app.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data via app settings (Settings → Delete Account) or by emailing support@gymologia.com.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: You may revoke location access permission at any time via your device settings. You may opt out of push notifications in your device notification settings.
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data by contacting support.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@gymologia.com.

9. Health and Fitness Data

Weight, height, and fitness goals are provided voluntarily by members. This data is used solely to display your personal progress within the app. It is visible to your assigned trainer and gym staff unless you opt out during membership onboarding (you can uncheck the monitoring consent option at any time via your profile).

We do not share your health data with any third-party organizations or advertisers.

10. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with their data, please contact us at support@gymologia.com and we will promptly delete the information.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in the following locations:

  • India — Primary API server (AWS, ap-south-1, Mumbai region)
  • Singapore — Database storage (Supabase)
  • United States — Push notification delivery (Firebase / Google)

All international transfers are made with appropriate safeguards, and service providers are contractually required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.

12. Cookies and Local Storage

The Gymologia mobile applications use secure local storage (on your device) only to maintain your session (login tokens) and your language preference. We do not use advertising cookies or track your activity across other websites or apps.

Our website (gymologia.com) may use minimal functional cookies. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

13. Notifications

We send push notifications for:

  • Announcements from your gym (sent by gym staff).
  • Platform notifications from Gymologia (e.g., new features, important policy updates).
  • Referral reward updates (for Gymologia Referral app users).

You may disable push notifications at any time via your device settings. Note that disabling notifications will prevent you from receiving gym announcements and membership updates.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated via in-app notifications. The updated policy will be posted at gymologia.com/privacy with the new effective date. Continued use of the Platform after notification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
