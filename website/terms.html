Subscriptions and payment

Gyms subscribe to Gymologia on a monthly plan, Basic or Pro. The price is shown in the app before you pay. New gym owners get one free trial, once per person — not once per gym. Payment is taken by Cashfree; we do not store your card details.

The gym tools in the app — members, staff, attendance, leave, events and chat — need an active trial or paid plan. Until a gym has one, its owner can only edit the gym's profile, choose a plan, or delete the gym.

A subscription runs to the end of the period you paid for. If it lapses, your gym stops appearing in member search, its public listing is withdrawn, and the gym tools become read-only: you can still see your member and staff records, but you cannot add or change anything until you renew. Your data is not deleted, so a billing lapse never locks you out of your own business records.
